Overview

The Eureka PowerSpeed sits in the middle of Eureka's budget upright range, priced at $75.99 with a 960-watt motor and a full accessory kit thrown in. Almost 17,700 Amazon ratings back it up, and the appeal is straightforward: more included tools than the cheaper NEU10AE5, without stepping up to pet-specific pricing.

What the Accessory Kit Actually Adds

The dusting brush, crevice tool and upholstery tool that ship with the PowerSpeed aren't unique to this model, but including all three at this price point is where the value shows up. The stretch hose is the more useful addition day to day: it extends the reach of the main unit for stairs, curtains and ceiling corners without detaching the whole vacuum head the way a canister design requires. It also handles carpet, hard floors and shag rugs without swapping attachments, which is more floor-type flexibility than the base NEU10AE5 offers.

Weight and Everyday Use

At 11.5 lbs, the PowerSpeed is noticeably heavier than a cordless stick vacuum, and heavier than Eureka's own NEU10AE5 at 7.7 lbs. That weight comes from the fuller motor and accessory set, and it's still light enough to carry up stairs, just not something you'd want to lift one-handed for long stretches. The foam filter is washable, keeping ongoing costs low, though it isn't true HEPA, so allergy sufferers should weigh that before buying.

Backed by a 2-Year Warranty

Eureka backs the PowerSpeed with a 2-year limited warranty, longer than what many budget uprights offer at this price. Combined with the size of its review base, nearly 17,700 ratings and counting, that's a reasonable amount of real-world reliability data to lean on before buying.

Eureka PowerSpeed vs. NEU10AE5 vs. NEU181A

All three are corded, bagless, and built on similar Eureka motors, so the choice comes down to what you need beyond basic suction. The NEU10AE5 is the cheapest at $59.98 but skips the motorized brush roll and extra tools. The PowerSpeed adds the stretch hose and full accessory kit for about $16 more. The NEU181A costs roughly $14 more again but swaps the general accessory kit for a dedicated Pet Turbo Tool built for fur and dander. Pick based on whether you need more tools (PowerSpeed) or pet-specific cleaning (NEU181A).
