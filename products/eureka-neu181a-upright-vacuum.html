Overview

The Eureka NEU181A is a corded, bagless upright built around one clear priority: pulling pet hair off furniture, stairs and carpet without charging a premium price. It pairs a 960-watt motor rated at 13 kPa of suction with a dedicated Pet Turbo Tool, a handheld attachment spun by airflow rather than the main motor, made specifically to lift embedded fur from upholstery and stairs rather than just skimming loose hair off the top. At $89.98, it sits in Eureka's budget upright lineup alongside the NEU10AE5, and the Pet Turbo Tool is the main reason to pick one over the other.

Suction and Filtration

13 kPa places the NEU181A in the same suction range as most vacuums under $100, and the 960-watt (8-amp) motor keeps it competitive with entry-level Shark and Bissell uprights at a similar price. The bigger factor for long-term performance is the washable foam filter. Instead of buying replacement filters every few months, you rinse the foam, let it dry fully for about 24 hours, then reinstall it. Skipping that step, or reinstalling it damp, is the most common reason bagless vacuums in this price range lose suction over time.

Eureka rates noise output at roughly 88 dB on full power, based on the manufacturer's own listing. That is louder than newer sealed-motor uprights, so it is worth factoring in if you tend to vacuum around sleeping kids or during work calls at home.

How It Handles Pet Hair

The Pet Turbo Tool is what separates this model from Eureka's base PowerSpeed line. It is an attachment for stairs, upholstery and car interiors, powered by the airflow passing through it rather than a motor of its own, which is a common and effective design for this price tier. The main floor brush roll, on the other hand, is not marketed with any specific anti-tangle feature on Eureka's spec sheet, so long hair can still wind around it over time. That maintenance step, pulling wrapped hair off the roller every so often, applies to most uprights in this price bracket that do not advertise a self-cleaning brush by name.

Weight, Handling and the 2.6-Liter Dust Cup

At 10 lbs, the NEU181A is on the heavier end for an upright, though still light enough to carry up a flight of stairs one-handed. Being corded is the trade-off for that lack of a battery: it runs for as long as it is plugged in, which matters more for a full-house clean than it does for quick touch-ups.

The 2.6-liter dust cup is genuinely larger than what most uprights in this size and price class offer. In practice, that means fewer trips to empty it during a single cleaning session, which adds up in a home with pets shedding daily.

Where It Falls Short

Three things to weigh before buying. It is corded only, with no cordless option anywhere in the NEU181A line, so anyone who wants to leave the wall outlet behind should look elsewhere. At roughly 88 dB, it runs louder than most newer sealed-motor designs. And the standard brush roll has no dedicated anti-tangle mechanism, so occasional hair removal from the roller stays part of the maintenance routine.

Eureka NEU181A vs. Eureka NEU10AE5: Which Should You Buy?

Both vacuums share the same core Eureka design: bagless, corded and similarly priced, with both currently listed as Amazon's Choice in their categories. The NEU10AE5 is Eureka's higher-volume general-purpose seller, while the NEU181A is built specifically around the Pet Turbo Tool. If pet hair on furniture, stairs or car seats is a daily reality, the NEU181A's extra tool earns its place. If your cleaning needs are mostly floors with no pets in the picture, the NEU10AE5 does the same core job for the same money. Read our full Eureka NEU10AE5 review for the complete side-by-side breakdown.

Who Should Buy the Eureka NEU181A

This is a solid pick for renters and pet owners who want a dependable corded upright without paying premium prices, and who do not mind trading cordless freedom for uninterrupted runtime and a lower price tag. It is not the right choice if a lightweight cordless stick vacuum is what you are actually after, or if a quiet motor matters more to you than suction power.
